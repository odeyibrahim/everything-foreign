Our Story

Built in the heart of Lagos, YENO LABEL is a contemporary fashion brand rooted in individuality, timeless versatility, and intentional design. We create limited-edition pieces for women with a strong sense of self—women who value comfort, elegance, and effortless style that transcends trends.

Guided by a "less is more" philosophy, every collection is thoughtfully designed with a deep appreciation for heritage, feminine expression, and meticulous craftsmanship. Our pieces are embedded with signature details and created to move seamlessly between the different worlds women navigate daily.

As advocates of slow fashion, we champion conscious investment in quality garments made to last beyond seasons. Each piece is crafted to the finest standard, offering timeless staples that remain relevant, versatile, and beautiful for years to come.

YENO LABEL is more than clothing—it is a sanctuary for women who seek style with depth, intention, and longevity.

"We believe the most sustainable garment is one you keep forever."

Sustainability

At YENO LABEL, sustainability is not a trend — it is the foundation of everything we create. We believe the most sustainable garment is one you keep forever. That is why every piece we make is designed with longevity in mind, crafted to the finest standard, built to outlast seasons, and styled to remain relevant long after the moment it was made.

We choose slow, on purpose. Fast fashion thrives on excess. We reject that. As advocates of slow fashion, we produce in limited editions—intentionally small runs that reduce waste, honour craftsmanship, and ensure that what we put into the world is worth keeping. We do not overproduce. We do not cut corners.

Care Guide

Your YENO LABEL piece is crafted to last. With the right care, it will remain a staple in your wardrobe for years to come.

Washing
Hand wash in cool water with a mild detergent. Do not wring or twist — gently press out excess water. Machine wash on delicate/gentle cycle only where the care label permits. Wash dark and light colours separately.
Drying
Lay flat to dry or hang in a well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight. Avoid tumble drying unless the care label states otherwise. Direct heat can damage fabric structure and colour.
Ironing
Iron on a low to medium heat setting. Use a pressing cloth between the iron and the garment to protect delicate fabrics. Steam ironing is recommended for structured pieces.
Storage
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Fold knitwear rather than hanging to prevent stretching. Use garment bags for long-term storage.

We encourage repair over replacement. If a seam comes loose or a button falls off, a skilled tailor can restore your piece with ease. For significant repairs, reach out to yenolabelhq@gmail.com.
